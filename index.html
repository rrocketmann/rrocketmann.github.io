Schoolargy

One of my more cruel creations, Schoolargy is an unblocked games website specifically masked as PowerSchool's Schoology learning management platform. Now with the rise of new tools such as GoGuardian and Classwize, older gaming websites with their original look tend to get blocked much quicker. Schoolargy avoids this by mimicking the exact visual design of Schoology, a platform that schools actively whitelist, making it appear as legitimate educational software to both filtering tools and teachers alike.
